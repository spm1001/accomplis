## For Claudes to come

A skill teaches through three channels, and only two were named before tonight. Content (what it says) and register (how it says it — P1–P8 in skill-forge's register-principles) were known; the third is **topology: what it enumerates**. An enumerated list silently declares the world closed, and models follow maps with capability-graded literalism — measured tonight at read_inbox 0% for skill-guided Sonnet 5 vs 100% for skill-guided Opus 5 and Fable on the same task (n=5/n=3; bare Sonnet also 100%, because unguided models sweep). The cheap fix is one floors-not-fences sentence ("the spots where the gold usually sits — a floor for your reading, not a fence around it"). The same disease infects LLM-judge rubrics: the eval's first judge zeroed fixture-accurate answers as "fabrication" for exceeding the rubric, one note literally catching itself mid-sentence ("wait, these actually match") and penalising anyway. And residence beats richness now has numbers, in both directions: identical grounding text produced 0% behaviour change from PATTERNS.md (an unloaded reference) and 80% from SKILL.md (the loaded file). Corollaries: control-test LLM judges with a known-rich positive before trusting a single score, and when a fix's proof metric exists deterministically (a call in a log), watch that — judge points can move for reasons that have nothing to do with your fix.
